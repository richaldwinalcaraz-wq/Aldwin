# Medical Spa Photography Brief — Master Guide

## OVERVIEW

This document provides detailed photography requirements for a luxury medical spa website. Each procedure requires authentic before/after photography that builds trust, showcases results, and converts visitors into booking consultations.

**Key Principle:** Real client results > Stock photography. Authenticity is paramount in aesthetic medicine.

---

## GENERAL PHOTOGRAPHY STANDARDS

### Before Photo Requirements
- **Lighting:** Natural, consistent lighting (no harsh shadows on face)
- **Angle:** Frontal, 45-degree side angle, and profile (3 angles minimum per client)
- **Expression:** Neutral, relaxed expression (no forced smile)
- **Distance:** Close-up enough to show detail, far enough for context
- **Background:** Plain white or light gray (minimizes distraction)
- **No Makeup:** Before photos should be bare-faced (shows true baseline)
- **No Filters:** Raw, unretouched image (shows real starting point)
- **Resolution:** Minimum 4000x3000px (can be cropped later)
- **Format:** RAW or high-quality JPG

### After Photo Requirements
- **Timing:** Minimum 2-4 weeks post-treatment (allows full results to show)
- **Lighting:** Identical to before photo (same time of day, same room)
- **Angle:** Exact same angles as before photo (left, front, right profile)
- **Expression:** Neutral, relaxed (same as before photo)
- **Makeup:** Can include light makeup (consistent with client's usual look)
- **No Filters:** Minimal retouching (remove blemishes only, no skin softening)
- **Background:** Same as before photo (for seamless comparison)
- **Resolution:** Minimum 4000x3000px
- **Format:** RAW or high-quality JPG

### Lighting Setup (Studio Photography)
- **Key Light:** Positioned at 45° angle, 3-4 feet from client
- **Fill Light:** Opposite side, slightly lower intensity (reduces shadows)
- **Backdrop:** Pure white or soft gray paper/fabric
- **Color Temperature:** 5000-5500K (daylight balanced)
- **No Shadows:** Shadows under chin/jaw should be minimal
- **Consistent:** All client photos must have identical lighting

### Post-Processing Standards
- **Color Correction:** Only correct white balance (no color grading)
- **Retouching:** Remove temporary blemishes ONLY
  - ✓ Acne spots, pimples
  - ✓ Temporary redness
  - ✗ Wrinkles, fine lines
  - ✗ Skin smoothing/softening
  - ✗ Texture alteration
- **Sharpening:** Minimal (maintain natural skin texture)
- **Before/After Sliders:** Crop to identical dimensions (same frame per pair)

### Client Privacy & Consent
- **Signed Release Form:** Before/after photos cannot be used without written consent
- **Anonymity Option:** Client name should be first name + age only (e.g., "Sarah, 42")
- **Diversity:** Ensure before/afters represent diverse skin tones, ethnicities, ages
- **Storage:** Keep all client data confidential (HIPAA compliance)

# PROCEDURE 1: BOTOX / ANTI-WRINKLE INJECTIONS

## Objective
Showcase wrinkle reduction, natural-looking results, maintained facial expression.

## Target Results to Photograph
- **Forehead Lines:** Horizontal lines reduction (frown relaxation)
- **Crow's Feet:** Fine lines around eyes (softened, not frozen)
- **Glabellar Lines:** Frown lines between eyebrows (smoothed)
- **Result Style:** Natural (not over-done, client can still move face)

## Before Photo Focus Areas
- **Close-up frontal:** Forehead wrinkles clearly visible
- **45° angles:** Show depth of crow's feet
- **Expression:** Neutral, raised eyebrows (show baseline wrinkle depth)
- **Upper face only:** Crop to forehead, eyes, brows (procedure area)

## After Photo Requirements
- **Timeline:** 2 weeks post-injection (full results visible)
- **Expression:** Same neutral with raised eyebrows (show result)
- **Lighting:** Identical to before (shows wrinkle reduction clearly)
- **Multiple angles:** Front + both profiles (crow's feet very visible on profiles)
- **Natural movement:** Client should be able to make expressions (not frozen)

## Client Diversity
- [ ] Age: 30s, 40s, 50s, 60s+
- [ ] Skin tone: Fair, medium, dark (show results across all skin types)
- [ ] Gender: Include both men and women
- [ ] Wrinkle severity: Mild, moderate, severe cases

## Specific Angles
1. **Frontal Neutral:** Looking straight at camera, relaxed
2. **Frontal Raised Brows:** Client raises eyebrows to show forehead movement (or lack thereof post-Botox)
3. **45° Left Profile:** Shows crow's feet area clearly
4. **45° Right Profile:** Shows crow's feet area clearly
5. **Three-Quarter Left:** Asymmetrical view of face
6. **Three-Quarter Right:** Asymmetrical view of face

## Environmental Photos Needed
- [ ] Botox injection being administered (side profile, showing needle insertion)
- [ ] Provider holding syringe over treatment area
- [ ] Botox vial + syringe on treatment table
- [ ] Client in consultation chair (post-treatment, happy expression)

## Success Metrics
- Wrinkles visibly softened (not eliminated)
- Client's expressions still natural (can smile, frown, raise brows)
- Before/after dramatic enough to convince prospects
- No frozen, over-done appearance

## Timeline Infographic
- **Immediate (Day 1):** "No visible results yet. Slight redness may occur."
- **Days 3-5:** "Subtle softening begins."
- **Week 1:** "Noticeable improvement in fine lines."
- **Week 2:** "Full results visible. Maximum effectiveness."
- **Lasts:** "Results last 3-4 months. Maintenance treatments recommended."

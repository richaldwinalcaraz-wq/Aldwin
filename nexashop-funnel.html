Questions

You probably want
to ask this.

If you don't see your question below, just message us — average reply time is under 2 hours.

Do you really take zero transaction fees?+

Yes. You pay one flat monthly subscription — that's it. We never deduct a percentage from your sales. Your payment processor (GCash, Maya, Stripe, etc.) charges their own standard rates, but Bodoir adds nothing on top.

Can I migrate from Shopify or Lazada?+

Yes — we have a one-click Shopify import tool and a CSV template for WooCommerce / Etsy exports. Your products, photos, variants, and historical order data all move over. Most brands are fully live within 20 minutes.

What payment methods can my customers use?+

Credit cards (Visa / Mastercard / Amex), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, ACH bank transfer, and Stripe. You can enable or disable any method per product or per storefront.

How does the drop campaign feature work?+

You set a live date, a stock quantity, and an optional waitlist. When the drop goes live, we handle the surge, prevent overselling in real time, and automatically notify your waitlist when items sell out. You can also schedule restocks.

Is there a free trial?+

Pro and Elite plans include a 14-day free trial — no credit card required. Starter plan has a 7-day trial. You won't be charged until you choose to continue, and you can cancel any time before that.

Can I use my own domain?+

All plans let you connect a custom domain. Elite plan additionally supports white-labeling — your checkout, emails, and customer-facing pages show only your brand with no Bodoir branding.
